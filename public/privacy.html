Privacy Policy

Welcome to Study Mode App Lock & Timer. We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle information within our application in strict compliance with the Google Play Store developer guidelines.

Our application is a fully functional offline-first utility designed to help students, professionals, and users avoid social media, gaming, and app distractions during scheduled focus times. Below we outline our direct compliance with sensitive permissions.

2. Information Collection & Use

We strongly believe in absolute physical data minimalism. To guarantee maximum user security, we collect no user data:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do NOT collect, transmit, store or share your name, email address, physical location, phone number, contacts, or browser history on external cloud servers.
  • Local Device Storage: All time tables, focus schedules, list of customized blocked apps, and break limit configurations are stored exclusively on your device's local database (Sandbox Local Preference Context). It never leaves your device.
  • No Database Sync or Telemetry: There is no telemetry, background analytics tracking, or remote cloud synchronization. Your application behaves fully locally and privately.

3. Sensitive Device Permissions

To provide real-time digital protection, app locks, and schedules, our Android app requires specialized system permissions. The nature and detailed use case for each of these permissions are detailed below:

Permission Type Purpose & How it works
Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) Critical Used strictly to detect when a blacklisted application package is launched into the active foreground. If a blocked app is detected during a scheduled study focus period, the accessibility event triggers our high-utility blocker overlay screen. Absolutely no personal input, password fields, or background content are recorded.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Critical Necessary to query and compile a healthy, localized list of currently installed applications on your device, allowing you to select and select which distracting apps to block.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Draw over other apps) Critical Allows the application to draw and present the highly functional full-screen study block overlay on top of selected applications, successfully locking the screen during study focus cycles to guarantee zero distractions.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Critical Starts and runs an active background foreground service. This keeps study mode timers running reliably without getting killed by the Android operating system task cleaner during multi-tasking or swipe events.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Normal Used to keep you informed of the active study schedule via persistent, clean system notifications, showing active states and emergency break timers.
Prominent Disclosure (Accessibility Service): Study Mode App Lock & Timer utilizes the Accessibility Service API solely to identify active application package runs dynamically. It is used exclusively to overlay block screens and support focus timers. This service is never utilized to read or harvest passwords, view messages, click buttons, or store private personal user information. It requires active user engagement to enable and can be deactivated anytime in Settings.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To ensure this focus utility remains free for students, we integrate Google AdSense / AdMob to display visual interstitial advertisements strictly when you take an emergency study break. These ads are completely in line with the Google Publisher Policies, run through secure secure sandboxes, and are non-invasive to your local storage systems.

5. Children's Privacy

This study application is built for student groups, teenagers, and adult focus enhancement. It does not solicit, request, or compile information from kids under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the standards set by children protection acts (such as COPPA) globally.
